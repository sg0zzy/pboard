# Pboard Privacy Policy

The policy is maintained in [docs/privacy.md](docs/privacy.md), which also provides the privacy page for GitHub Pages.

The published policy covers the account inbox and the relay's data handling: what the relay stores (sealed
operations, opaque device keys and key envelopes, and hints addressed by an account hash) and what it never holds
(board plaintext, content keys, Google credentials, or email addresses for the inbox).
